BTU Lab Design System

Colors, type, spacing, and components for the BTU Lab website redesign. Built around heat, combustion, and char: maroon, red, and orange on an off-white ground.

Accent on Highlight 3.6:1 AA large only Borders, focus ring, large labels
Secondary on Neutral 3.5:1 AA large only Large headings on dark surfaces

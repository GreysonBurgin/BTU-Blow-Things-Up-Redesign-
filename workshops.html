Workshops

Show Us. And Tell Us.

BTU Lab hosts all kinds of workshops! Whether you're a student, instructor, local artist, or too cool for labels, come share your skills in two easy steps:

  1. Fill out this basic proposal form.
  2. Email us at atlasbtulab@gmail.com to let us know you did.

That's it! We will get back to you with confirmation as soon as possible.

This site is a student class project redesigning the BTU Lab website for a CU Boulder course. It is not the lab's official site. The official site is at sites.google.com/view/btu-lab.
